HARDWARE DESIGN VAULT

24 V I/O, Protection, Power & PCB

Digital output design

Topology

Logic-level N-channel power MOSFET, low-side/sinking: +24 V to load to drain; source to 0 V.

Gate

Gate network must guarantee OFF during reset/startup. Final values depend on MOSFET, switching need and MCU drive.

Inductive loads

Use suitable flyback/transient suppression. 1N4148 is a small-signal diode, not the default power-load flyback device.

Selection

Check VDS transient margin, RDS(on) at actual gate voltage, SOA, thermal dissipation and transient robustness, not headline current alone.

Candidate

IRLB8742 is available for evaluation but is not frozen.

Power, EMI & reliability

24 V power

Use protected 24-to-logic conversion. Never apply 24 V directly to Mega logic supply. Design field-current and sensitive logic return paths deliberately.

EMI technique

Keep switching loops short, suppress inductive transients near their source, decouple IC supplies, and evaluate bulk capacitance at power-entry/load-step locations.

Input technique

24 V inputs require conditioning/protection. Debounce slow mechanical contacts without erasing valid pulses; high-speed signals require timer-aware hardware acquisition.

Safe startup

Outputs must not unpredictably energize during reset, boot, watchdog recovery or communication loss. Each fault mode requires defined behavior.

PCB review

Review power entry, protection, connector current, copper thermal capacity, return paths, test points, programming access, isolation boundaries and serviceability.

Component discipline

Do not freeze parts from seller titles. Use exact manufacturer datasheets and distinguish candidate, prototype-tested and frozen-production status.
