SYSTEM ENGINEERING

Motion, Drive, Vision, HMI, SCADA & I4.0

ST-MOTION / ST-DRIVE

Feedback technique

Time-only motor positioning is open-loop and varies with load, supply, friction and inertia. Use encoder feedback where repeatability matters.

ST-MOTION

PLC owns sequence and target intent; motion control owns time-critical pulse/Step-Dir generation, positioning and future coordinated motion.

ST-DRIVE

Closed-loop drive direction with dedicated MCU, encoder feedback, position/speed feedback, following-error and stall diagnostics.

ST-VISION

Chip / Crystal Inspection

  • Synchronize capture to indexing.
  • Investigate global shutter and hardware trigger.
  • Control strobe/backlight, exposure and ROI.
  • Inspect presence, edge chip/breakage, optically detectable cracks, dimension and orientation.
  • Return PASS/FAIL plus measurement/orientation data.
  • No speed claim before hardware benchmark.

Pad Alignment & Dispensing

  • Image pad/frame before dispense.
  • Determine center, X/Y offset and rotation.
  • Apply camera-to-machine transform.
  • VISION to PLC decision to MOTION correction to dispensing.
  • Future post-dispense inspection can verify deposit geometry.

Reliability

Control illumination/exposure before increasing algorithm complexity. Calibrate image-to-machine coordinates and physically benchmark latency, repeatability and false decisions.

ST-HMI / ST-SCADA

Authority

HMI Object to HMI Tag to PLC Logical Data to PLC/HMI Interface to CPU to I/O Mapper/Module Manager to hardware. PLC remains authoritative.

Commands

Normal HMI writes command M/D data; ladder/interlocks decide Y outputs. FORCE Y remains a separate engineering function.

SCADA

Restricted ST PLC supervisory layer for visualization, alarms, trends, diagnostics and future historian functions, not deterministic machine-control authority.

ST-I4.0 PLC

Main CPU

STM32H723ZGT6 is the current primary candidate; exact board and production hardware are not frozen.

Connectivity

ESP32-C6 may serve as non-authoritative wireless/IIoT companion. Connectivity failure should not stop deterministic PLC scanning.

Layering

Process to ST I/O/MOTION/DRIVE to PLC/HMI/VISION to SCADA/historian/EDGE to MES/reports/analytics/cloud. Critical control remains local.
